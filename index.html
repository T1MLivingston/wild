Rules

  • Students ride the bus both ways and must be picked up at SSCS at 4:00 PM. No pickup at Wild Florida.
  • Any money owed to the school must be paid first.
  • Electronics are brought at the student's own risk.
  • One adult chaperone for each group of 7 to 11 students.

Eligibility

Students who receive a disciplinary referral or suspension within one month (30 calendar days) prior to a scheduled field trip will not be permitted to attend. For disciplinary referrals or suspensions occurring more than 30 days prior to the field trip, eligibility will be determined at the discretion of school administration.

FAQ

Can I pick my child up at the park?

No. Every student rides the bus both ways. Pickup is at SSCS at 4:00 PM.

My child has allergies. What should I do?

Note them on the permission slip, then send me a quick email so I know too.

Can I chaperone?

I'd love the help! Check the chaperone box on the permission slip, then fill out the chaperone sign-up form (opens in a new tab). Approved volunteers only. I'll follow up with you.

What if we can't afford it?

Please email me. Anyone can sponsor a classmate's trip, and I want every 6th grader there. See the sponsor option.

Can I pay late?

No. Payment closes Tue, Dec 1, and we can't take late payments.

What if it rains?

If anything changes, I'll email families.
